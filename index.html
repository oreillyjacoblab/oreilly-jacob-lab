Primary Care Workforce Policy Lab

How nurse practitioners are shaping the future of primary care.

Our lab studies the primary care nurse practitioner workforce, value-based payment models, and emerging models of care such as NP-owned practices and home-based primary care, using national Medicare claims analyses and statewide surveys.

Governance & Regulation

Does state scope-of-practice authority determine how NPs actually practice?

About

Monica O'Reilly-Jacob, PhD, APRN, FNP-BC, FAAN, FAANP, is a family nurse practitioner and nursing health services researcher at Columbia University School of Nursing. Her research examines the primary care nurse practitioner workforce, value-based payment models, and emerging models of care such as NP-owned practices and home-based primary care, drawing on national Medicare claims analyses and statewide surveys. She currently leads a multidisciplinary team studying NP-owned practice readiness for value-based payment, funded by the American Nurses Foundation.
